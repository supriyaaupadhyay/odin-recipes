Here is the recipe for Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • 1 cup khoya (mawa)
  • ¾ cup grated paneer
  • 2 tbsp semolina (rava)
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp cardamom powder
  • 1 tbsp milk (adjust as needed)
  • 1.75 cups sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 2–3 tbsp rose water
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Mash khoya until smooth, then mix in paneer, semolina, flour, baking powder, and cardamom.
  2. Add milk gradually to form a soft dough. Rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Prepare sugar syrup by boiling sugar and water until sticky, then add rose water.
  4. Shape dough into small balls without cracks.
  5. Fry on low heat until golden brown, rotating for even color.
  6. Drain and immediately soak hot jamuns in warm sugar syrup.
